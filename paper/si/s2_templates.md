## S2. Task templates

A template fixes what is asked; a seeded generator fills in the structure, the residues, the
colours and the wording. The corpus has {{corpus.n_templates}} templates. No template supplies more than
{{corpus.max_template_share_other:.0%}} of a category's tasks, except in name resolution (there
are only five ways to name a protein) and in the small call-list category of requests with
several right answers.
