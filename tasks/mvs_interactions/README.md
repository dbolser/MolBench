# Interactions tier (MVS track)

Generated by `scripts/author_interaction_tasks.py`; do not edit by hand. Named atoms
and a measurement or contact between them. The prompt names the atoms (PDB atom names,
author residue numbers), so the reference is fully determined; the script finds the
pairs in the frozen mmCIF with gemmi and writes each measured distance into
`provenance.measured` (37 measured lines in this tier, each end checked to
resolve to exactly one atom of the frozen atom table).

The human raters of the first study could not see what an interaction reference was
about (it was not framed), so every contact task here asks for the view to be centred
on the atoms involved, and the reference has that camera focus. The C-alpha distance
and transparency templates have no focus, because their subject is the whole molecule.

## How each reference is derived

| template | reference |
|---|---|
| `in-ca-distance` | Two residues of one chain, by seed (10 or more apart in sequence, CA atoms 6-30 A apart); the line joins the two CA atoms. |
| `in-computed` | The only copy of a named ligand with Mol*'s computed non-covalent interactions switched on for it, and the camera on it. |
| `in-disulfide` | One `disulf` record of the facts; SG-SG re-measured (1.9-2.3 A). |
| `in-hbond` | A side-chain donor and acceptor atom of two residues 2.5-3.0 A apart (gemmi neighbour search; donors: side-chain N, hydroxyl O of Ser/Thr/Tyr; acceptors: side-chain O, His ring N; not a charge pair; at least three residues apart). One pair per entry, by seed. |
| `in-ligand-contact` | A ligand N or O atom and a protein N or O atom 2.5-3.2 A apart (neighbour search; not two plain acceptor oxygens), for the only copy of a named ligand. One contact per entry, by seed. |
| `in-metal-contact` | The `metalc` records between the entry's only copy of a metal ion (or the iron of its only heme) and polymer atoms; neighbour search within 2.6 A, with symmetry, must agree. One line per bond. |
| `in-salt-bridge` | A Lys NZ or Arg NE/NH1/NH2 and an Asp or Glu carboxylate oxygen 2.5-3.2 A apart (neighbour search). One pair per entry, by seed. |
| `in-transparent` | Protein surface with the stated opacity (0.3, 0.4 or 0.5) over the only copy of a named ligand in ball-and-stick. |

Only residues that can be named without doubt are used: standard amino acids, full
occupancy, no alternate conformer. A measured line is graded by the two atoms it joins
(either direction); its label text, dash length and colour are styling and are not
graded. What Mol* draws for `in-computed` is the viewer's own computation; the graded
fact is that the display is switched on for the right atoms.
